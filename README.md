# Hi, I'm Robert (hoizi89)

I build open-source tools for [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/): cards and integrations around solar, batteries, heat pumps and the small things that make a house run. Built on night shifts, tested in my own home in Austria.

## Energy

- **[Power Origin](https://github.com/hoizi89/power-origin-card)** — a Lovelace card that answers one question first: where is the house's power coming from right now. Ring, columns, the day, the battery's night, and what the day earned. HACS.
- **[PV Management](https://github.com/hoizi89/pv_management)** — integration for variable and spot tariffs (aWATTar, smartENERGY, Tibber) with battery management, EPEX Spot, Solcast, energy benchmark and amortization tracking.
- **[PV Management Fix](https://github.com/hoizi89/pv_management_fix)** — the same for fixed-price tariffs: amortization, ROI, electricity quota, daily costs.

## House

- **[Advanced Switches](https://github.com/hoizi89/advanced_switches)** — virtual devices from smart plugs with energy metering and schedules.
- **[Lambda Heat Pumps](https://github.com/hoizi89/lambda_heat_pumps)** — integration for Lambda heat pumps.
- **[Water sensing](https://github.com/hoizi89/wasser_vibration)** — water flow detection from vibration (ADXL345), and a [wM-Bus companion](https://github.com/hoizi89/wasser_residuum).
- **[Waste collection](https://github.com/hoizi89/hacs_waste_card)** — a card for collection dates, with an [integration for Kirchham](https://github.com/hoizi89/hacs_kirchham_waste).

## Around it

- **[HA for Stream Deck](https://github.com/hoizi89/ha-for-streamdeck)** — show and control Home Assistant from an Elgato Stream Deck, no build step.
- **[Blueprints](https://github.com/hoizi89/ha-blueprints)** — a small collection of automations.

Bug reports and ideas are welcome in each repository's issues, in English or German.
